## 🇨🇳 中文

给 Facebook、Instagram、TikTok、Pinterest、YouTube 上以视觉为主的社交贴写文案。输入是视觉素材（图、视频、Reel、Short、pin）加目标平台、形式和目标；输出是符合各平台原生规则的文案，走 hook → payoff → CTA 结构，并套上各平台的 hashtag 规则。长度按每个 surface 校准：Pinterest 标题与描述不同、YouTube 标题与长视频描述不同、Reel 与 feed 不同、Facebook 与 IG 也不同。它把 Pinterest 当搜索引擎而不是 feed 来写，并强制为图片补 alt text。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 输入图/视频/Reel/Short/pin + 平台 + 目标 → 输出 hook + payoff + CTA 文案
- 按平台锁定长度：Facebook 照片 40-80 字符、社群贴 300-500；Instagram 照片 80-300、carousel 200-800、Reel 100-300；TikTok 通常 150 字符内
- hashtag 规则分平台：Facebook 1-3、Instagram 3-10、TikTok 3-5（大众 + niche + 具体）
- 处理 Instagram 链接不可点击，改用 link in bio 或 Story link sticker，并照顾 125 字符截断
- 把 Pinterest 当搜索引擎写标题与描述，而不是当 feed
- 写 YouTube 标题与长视频描述、Shorts 文案，图片必配 alt text

## 🇬🇧 English

Writes captions for visual-first social posts on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest and YouTube. The input is visual material (image, video, Reel, Short, pin) plus target platform, format and goal; the output follows each platform's native rules and runs on a hook, payoff and CTA structure with the matching hashtag policy. Length is calibrated per surface: Pinterest title versus description, YouTube title versus long-form description, Reel versus feed, Facebook versus Instagram. It treats Pinterest as a search engine rather than a feed and always writes alt text for images.

### ✨ What it does

- Image, video, Reel, Short or pin plus platform and goal in, hook + payoff + CTA copy out
- Length locked per platform: Facebook photo 40-80 characters and community posts 300-500; Instagram photo 80-300, carousel 200-800, Reel 100-300; TikTok usually within 150
- Per-platform hashtag rules: Facebook 1-3, Instagram 3-10, TikTok 3-5 (broad plus niche plus specific)
- Handles Instagram's non-clickable links with link in bio or a Story link sticker, and respects the 125-character truncation
- Writes Pinterest titles and descriptions for search, not as a feed
- Writes YouTube titles, long-form descriptions and Shorts copy, and always adds alt text for images
